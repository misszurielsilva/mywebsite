Instead of using ChatGPT I will be finding alternate resources to use in it's stead! Here is an outline of my replacement assignment.
Part 1: Find and link alternate resources to make a lesson plan and then explain why each is useful.
Part 2: Find and link alternate resources to find research papers from and explain why each is useful.
Part 3: Find and link other useful resources for computation and explain why each is useful.
 
 
 
 
 
Part 1:

Math Medic: Math Medic is a great resource if you want to find free lesson plans! They provide both learning targets and how to achieve those learning targets, including things like notes for teachers and actual worksheets. They also split up subjects into sections so if you wanted to use math medic for an entire unit it would be very possible!

Teachers Pay Teachers: If you're willing to spend some money Teachers Pay Teachers can also be a great resource! Even if not you can filter for free resources only! The resources here are also made by teachers for other teachers so you can support and recieve support from fellow teachers!  
 
 
 
 
Part 2:

OneSearch: If you go onto the CSUN Library website you will be able to find OneSearch! It let's you look for both actual books but papers and journals. The resources here are free through CSUN and lets you easily comb through all the resources all of CSUN's various subscriptions provide you! Most college library websites have a similar search like this one.
Google Scholar: Google Scholar works similarly to the normal Google search function but instead lets you look at papers and journals. Sometimes a resource may be behind a payway but if you look up the title on OneSearch you may be able to find it for free through CSUN. The search still provides many free readings so its a great resource to use
 
 
 
 
 
Part 3:

WolframAlpha: WolframAlpha is a great website if you want to quickly work through a problem. You can input a variety of functions and equations and it will solve it for you! The free version breaks down the process into a couple steps which are helpful if you wanted to more quickly go through a lenghty computation. It is also great for checking your work!
Desmos: Desmos is a great website to quickly graph functions, both 2D and their newer 3D modeling. It even has a function which allows you to create a variables beyong x and and y and quickly see what effect variations in them have on the graph. Its also great for checking your work as you can quickly see trends in graphs as well as intercepts.
