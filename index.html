Introduction!
 


 
 
 
 
 
 
Hello everyone my name is Zuriel Silva!
I got my bachelors of arts with a mathematics major and computer science minor May 2026 from Scripps College.
I have a particularly strong interest in number theory and the history of mathematics.
While often seen as a subject done alone mathematics is an incredibly collaborative practice. The ways both formulas and people connect through it are what make it so fascinating to me, and this aspect is something I hope to eventually share in my classroom.
 
Click here for my resume!
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
I think a lot of the time people tend to forget mathematicians are people rather than calculators. With this comes a large amount of drama happening between mathematicians.
In truth, my intro to history of mathematics was learning about the conflict between Newton and Leibniz when I was in high school. I soon realizing most mathematicians have a long series of relationships with one another, both positive and negative.
I hope by seeing that these highly regarded mathematicians, have drama just like us "regular folk," we can see math as a more approachable subject as it was built by people not so different from us! :]
